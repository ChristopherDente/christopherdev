Frequently Asked Questions

Are you open to freelance work?

Yes! I frequently take on freelance and consulting projects, particularly those involving computer vision pipelines, custom AI model integration, and high-performance WebSockets. If you have a specific project scope, please email me with the details and a timeline.

Can I contribute to your open-source projects?

Absolutely. ChristopherDev is built on the philosophy of collaborative learning. Feel free to fork any of my repositories on GitHub, create a feature branch, and submit a Pull Request. I review community contributions weekly.

Do you offer mentorship?

I do try to make time for the community. While my schedule can be tight, I'm always happy to answer specific technical questions or provide architectural advice over email or LinkedIn messages. Don't hesitate to reach out!

How quickly do you respond?

I aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 to 48 hours. If your request is urgent (e.g., critical bug in a production environment using my open-source code), please prefix your subject line with [URGENT].
